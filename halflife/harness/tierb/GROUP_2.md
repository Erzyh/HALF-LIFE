## Observations

- Alias resolution. 18 of the 50 texts contain a bare 'GPT-4 Turbo' / 'gpt-4-turbo' string. Most resolve to gpt-4-turbo-2024-04-09 by the date rule, but two papers pin it to the retired 0125-preview (W7155654136, W4408749848), and several 2025-2026 papers never state a snapshot at all (W4406058562, W4415372210, W4411453360, W7166547843 via an institutional proxy). In six papers the CSV live ID is only a citation or a related-work mention rather than a model that was run (gpt-4-turbo in W7158422658 and W4411119263, o4-mini in W4414992335, gpt-4-turbo in W4404261523, W4417258556, W7167069038); in those cases the record documents the model that was actually run, which is sometimes an unversioned 'GPT-4o'.
- Judge, annotator and teacher roles. Five papers use the live model only to label, grade or generate training data (W4403585459, W4411113160, W4389714070, W7136200360, W4403810019), so no reported number belongs to it. Several subject-role claims depend on auxiliary models that are not in the live list: gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18 as judge (W4416035496), gpt-4o-2024-08-06 as simulator and evaluator (W4403795327) or pipeline stage (W7158422658), gpt-5.1 as judge (W7201914963), Gemini-2.5-Flash / Qwen3-32B for answer mapping (W7140138327), and a proprietary Databricks judge (W4404354805). For W7140138327 and W4399911213 the recorded claim was chosen so that no LLM judge is needed.
- Data closure. Ten papers are blocked or weakened by inputs that cannot be obtained: copyrighted novels (W4404783080), Books3 snippets (W7166806371), PhysioNet-controlled clinical text (W7155452288, W4405173269), CRSP/Compustat market data (W7155654136), tweets (W4415372210), an internal Databricks benchmark (W4404354805), a restricted exam corpus (W7167069038), and unreleased curated sets (W4411453360, W7136200360). Two more are partial: LAB-Bench averages a private split into its numbers (W4402345509) and the Eurobarometer microdata need GESIS registration (W4403666370).
- Agentic pipelines. Eight papers report the live model's number through a multi-stage agent or tool harness (W7158422658, W4403885480, W4404260877, W4403707291, W4403795327, W4412945557, W7130617888, W4411453360). Prompts are usually printed, but the number also depends on loops, tools, VMs or simulators. These are recorded as 'maybe' when data are public and the pipeline is fully described, and 'ineligible' when the number exists only in figures or relies on dead auxiliary models.
- Numbers that exist only in figures or deltas. Six papers give the live model's result graphically or as a difference (W4399316968, W7130617888, W4403795327, W7160488761, W4411453360, W7166547843); they carry `no_number` even where prompt and data would otherwise qualify. W4399316968 also has a title mismatch: the text file is arXiv 2405.20362 (legal research tools, Magesh et al.), not the CSV title.
- Safety and terms of service. Four papers require eliciting harmful or malicious behaviour (W4402588152 jailbreaks, W4416035496 meme-based harmful instructions, W4412945557 OS sabotage / prompt injection, W4403795327 malicious simulated users). W7201914963 evaluates real transcripts about delusions and self-harm; it is blocked by its judge rather than by ToS.
- Cost profile of the eligible set. Low: W7117377764 (129 calls), W4399911213 (720). Mid: W7166808082 (306 image calls to o3), W4415196225 (858), W4411119263 (6,180 short calls), W7140138327 (3,777 gpt-5-mini calls). High: W4417257719 (long Japanese filings), W7164447524 (high-reasoning GPT-5, ~6k output tokens per item), W4414992335 (7,644 o3 image calls; a single-domain subset would be mid). Several eligible claims still need a scorer to be rebuilt from the paper (TURTLEAI turtle emulator, Hummus LaBSE similarity for non-classification tasks, Virginia great-circle error).
