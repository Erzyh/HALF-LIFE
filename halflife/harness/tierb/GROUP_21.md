## Observations

- Mention-only dominates: 26 of the 32 ineligible papers use GPT-4-Turbo/GPT-4o/o-series/GPT-5 only in a generative-AI-use disclosure (writing/grammar/translation assistance), a reference-list citation, or a background/survey sentence (role C), with no experiment of their own. Most of these are medicine, biology, or social-science papers (MED/LIFE/SOC groups) unrelated to LLM evaluation; the live id is simply the tool the authors used to polish their manuscript.
- Pre-existing/borrowed data, not fresh calls: four papers (W4412888223, W4415159397, W7164973659, W7166832820) analyze already-collected leaderboard or benchmark artifacts (AlpacaEval 2.0 cached completions, Chatbot Arena Elo scores, AppWorld trajectories) that technically include a tracked live model, but the authors state or imply they never called it themselves, so no prompt-level re-execution is possible from the paper alone.
- Role A (judge/annotator/data-generator only) accounts for 6 ineligible papers (W4407208106, W4403364995, W4416037518, W4412886850, W7203475044, and the classifier use in W4399115105): the live model produces captions, paraphrases, synthetic training data, or judgments feeding a separate analysis, with no capability metric of its own.
- Data closure blocks otherwise clean records: W4416037518 (proprietary business datasets), W4405933891 (an institution's internal curriculum-indicator database), and W4412465533 (a custom, unreleased simulated CT phantom image set) all have a dated live model, a real role-S/B claim, and in two cases a fully quoted prompt, but fail on data availability.
- tos: one paper (W4409362915) is otherwise a model example of clean methodology (dated gpt-4-turbo-2024-04-09/gpt-4o-2024-05-13, full appendix, Table-based ASR) but its claim is a jailbreaking/training-data-extraction attack, so it is blocked on principle rather than on missing information.
- Text-availability gaps: three papers (W4401742869, W7164395320, and implicitly W4413544104's supplementary prompt) could only be retrieved as an abstract/landing-page or an acknowledgments line pointing to a supplementary file; the live model and even the headline metric are named in prose, but no table or appendix was present in the extracted text to cite.
- Cost skews high for o-series/GPT-5 and long-context or long-prompt claims (W7164738679 multi-turn negotiation, W7164700917 up to 4M-token contexts, W4412585228 rubric-length grading prompts over 6,345 items, W7138918687 continuous traffic-simulation decisions, W7162606037's 20k-continuation validation run) and stays low for short single-call classification/generation tasks on gpt-4-turbo-2024-04-09 (W4409361695, W4411272344, W4404261042 all under $5 at 3 seeds).
- Alias resolution: every eligible/maybe gpt-4-turbo case resolved via an in-text date pin (2024-04-09) rather than the bare-alias date rule, except W4403705266 ("GPT4 April update", inferred) and W7138918687/W7162606037 (undated, resolved by the post-2024-07-01 rule). No paper in this group required the dead-preview branch of the alias rule as its primary live model.
