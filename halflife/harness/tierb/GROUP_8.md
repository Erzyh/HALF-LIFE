## Observations

- Role distribution: S 26, B 7, A 3, C 14. In 14 of 50 papers the live ID appears only in related work, a model-history sentence, a tool list, or as a benchmark's fixed reference generator (AlpacaEval 2); a further 3 use it only as a judge, pseudo-labeler or data synthesizer. The string match on 'GPT-4 Turbo' is the main source of these false positives.
- Among the 33 papers that actually ran the live model (S/B), the prompt is printed in full in 12, partially (template with unstated demonstrations or fields) in 11, and not at all in 10. Prompts that exist only in an appendix missing from the extracted text (AnalogCoder, the African-languages paper) or in supplementary material (cell detection) count as not printed.
- Closed inputs are the dominant hard blocker for the medical, industrial and finance papers: data_closed appears 12 times (patient vignettes and CT reports, hospital prescriptions, proprietary exam questions, plant photographs, VC watchlists, licensed financial databases); tos appears 2 times (X/Twitter posts).
- Alias resolution by publication date alone would have been wrong twice: W4405033839 states a knowledge cutoff of April 30, 2023 (a preview-snapshot cutoff) and W7119538517 evaluated a January 2024 question set against Gemini-Pro 1.0; both are recorded as dead-preview despite 2025/2026 publication dates. W4402670994 swaps the GPT-4-Turbo and GPT-4o snapshot IDs in its model table.
- The eligible/maybe set is cost-heavy: 5 of 10 are high tier because the number is produced by an agentic loop (MALADE, FEVER multi-hop search), a stochastic simulation with hundreds of calls per realization (opinion dynamics), or long code/few-shot prompts (SciCode, RobustUIE). Only the Tylenol misinformation and rumor-detection claims are low tier.
- Two papers ran a possibly callable model that the ID scan did not flag: FinVerBench (W7162893340) evaluated GPT-4.1 through the OpenRouter alias openai/gpt-4.1 with printed prompts and public data, and the nephrology paper (W4417152279) evaluated GPT-5 under the malformed string 'gpt-5-2025-04-16'. Both records note this; FinVerBench is a re-screen candidate if gpt-4.1 is treated as live.
- Text-file quality: all 50 files are the intended papers. W4413259164 lacks its front matter (starts at the Introduction), W4403815758 has lost inter-word spaces, and several ACL tables (W4402670994, W4412889855, W4403556032) are extracted as merged number strings that had to be split by column order.
