Observations

- Role is again the main filter: 18 of 50 papers use the live id only as a citation, a pricing reference or a documentation URL (role C: W7204543494, W7134045725, W4406121825, W4409854784, W4416096257, W4416036791, W7147716548, W4403579486, W7202072803, W4417144639, W4410607707, W4411120928, W4406603690) or as judge, parser, reward annotator or data generator (role A: W4402670152, W4406302175, W4403364826, W4411120613, W4402699852). Three of the C papers are reviews or position papers (W7147716548, W4417144639, W4410607707) and one analyzes a synthetic Kaggle table without any model call (W7202072803).
- Catalog gaps in the live_ids column: the screener listed only gpt-4-turbo for Nunchi-Bench (W4412888118) and Genome-Bench (W4415145303), but the papers also run dated subjects that are still callable (GPT-4o (20240513); o1 12-17-24), which were used for the records. Conversely, several papers' real subject is a model outside the catalog (gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18 in W4406302175 and W4406603690; gpt-4o-2024-08-06 in W4409854784; gpt-4-vision-preview in W4406121825), so those records are judged on the listed id only.
- Alias resolution: bare 'GPT-4 Turbo' in papers dated after 2024-07-01 (W4416034777, W4412889033, W4400434899, W4406302175) was resolved to gpt-4-turbo-2024-04-09 by the SCHEMA rule; W4400434899 (arXiv 2024-07-05) is borderline and may have used a preview snapshot. Dead previews are explicit in W4402670152 (gpt-4-turbo-preview as subject), W7118639952 ('version released January 2024') and, by provenance of copied ProxyQA rows, W4416036791.
- The eight eligible records are small, self-contained and cheap: game or decision prompts with no dataset (W4416034777, W4400434899: 100 runs each), released benchmarks with printed MCQ/classification prompts (W7135428702 SommBench, W4412888118 Nunchi-Bench, W4404781531 MalAlgoQA, W4407571428 PhysiCo, W7166799942 MELD text-only) and a released annotated tweet set (W4412889033). Only PhysiCo and MalAlgoQA reach the mid tier; none needs an LLM judge.
- 'maybe' verdicts are driven by a single soft dependency rather than a hard blocker: upstream preprocessing outside the paper (BM25 candidate sets in W4407683641, OCR of court files in W4404518515, ALCE retrieval files in W4399912745, video frame sampling in W7161451968, code-execution harness in W4392822190), abbreviated or described-only prompts (W4405425460, W7164614711, W4415145303), or unreleased labels for a public corpus (W7166528822, W4404782619). Six of the eleven sit in the high cost tier because of long inputs or o-series pricing.
- Hard blockers among role-S/B papers are data closure (licensed abstracts W7167269259, hospital reports W4409854784, ACR exam items W4417216433, hidden competition labels W4403444154, unreleased simulator benchmark W7203697962), prompts left to supplementary files (W7167269259, W7159734092, W4406840428, W4404987361, W4412886705), web-interface use (W4416096257, W7118639952, W7167411573) and human-rated outcomes (W7166585710, W7167411573). Multi-model simulation pipelines (W4416223237 with a gpt-4o user simulator and a reward-model scorer; W7159734092 with a gpt-5.4 judge) are not light re-executions even where the subject is callable.
