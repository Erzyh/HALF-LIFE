Common patterns:
1. "gpt-4-turbo" census hits are mostly alias hits: 4 of 12 papers actually ran gpt-4-1106/0125-preview (dead), 2 used the ChatGPT web UI, 1 used gpt-4-turbo only for data construction — only 3 papers ran a still-callable dated snapshot (2 × gpt-4-turbo-2024-04-09, 1 × gpt-4o-2024-05-13).
2. Where the outcome is human (belief change, expert/panel ratings, interpretive coding: 5 papers), only a model-side proxy is re-executable; the survivable claims are the purely automatic ones (correlation with expert lists, cultural-map distance, tag effect, classifier F1).
3. Artifact quality is bimodal: several repos release every model response (news ratings, DAT, voting, tag probes) but 5 of 9 repos/OSF projects carry no license, and the datasets that gate re-execution (IVS, NHS/Reddit/diaries, PATIENT-Ψ-CM, Alexander Street transcripts) are third-party or restricted.
