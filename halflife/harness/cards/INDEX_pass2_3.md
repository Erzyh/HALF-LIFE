## Common patterns

1. **Three of four "gpt-4-turbo" census hits are false positives, each from a different mechanism.** W4412944944 matched a bibliography entry ("OpenAI. 2024. Hello, gpt-4 turbo"), W4415308862 matched dataset provenance (GPT-4 Turbo wrote some MT-Bench responses), W4411119263 matched a description of prior work (WildBench's checklists). Only W4411119263 has a genuine retiring arm, and it is a *different* model (gpt-4o-2024-05-13, spelled out with its date in the appendix). Two of the four papers also had their true live arm invisible to a text census: W7148175927's gpt-5-mini judge is named only in an appendix table, and its gpt-4.1-nano use is visible only in the repository.
2. **Artifact quality and study relevance are uncorrelated.** The most complete artifact in the batch (W4415308862: prompts, data, runners, aggregated analysis and 2.5 GB of raw transcripts under Apache-2.0) is blocked because no arm is retiring; the paper with the biggest reported contribution (W4412944944, ACL 2025 long) shipped an empty repository. Licences are the recurring weak point: two of the three non-empty repositories (ReIFE, future-aligned-proposals) carry no licence at all.
3. **The intermediate artifact, not the prompt, is the wall for judge-robustness claims.** W7148175927 releases its inputs (2,823 SFT + 819 test instances) and its judge prompt but not the model outputs being judged, so a faithful Table 7 re-run first requires regenerating proposals — and the shipped SFT traces are gpt-5 distillation, which forces the card down to the two training-free systems. The same shape appears in W4411119263, where proprietary evaluators' item-level predictions were withheld while all 25 open-source ones were published, capping any comparison at one-decimal accuracy.
4. **Retiring reasoning-model arms run unparameterised.** In W7148175927 the shipped helper (`utils/api.py`) branches on the model name and drops `temperature` and `max_tokens` for gpt-5-family calls, so the gpt-5-mini judge ran at endpoint defaults while the gpt-4.1-mini judge ran at temperature 0.1 — an uncontrolled reasoning-effort parameter sitting inside the paper's own robustness check.
