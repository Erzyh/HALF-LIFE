## Batch notes

1. **Three of four repositories are real and complete; the gaps are in the glue, not the science.** Every paper released code under MIT (MedGENIE has no licence file at all) and every prompt that the authors wrote themselves is in-repo. What is missing in two cases is the driver that turns released prompts into the published number: Qworld ships both judge prompts and both schemas but nothing that loops them over expert-vs-generated criteria, and MedGENIE's judge prompts are not the authors' at all — they belong to an unpinned `ragas` install, which rewrote its metric prompts between 0.1.x and 0.2.x. Both cards therefore need a declared *pipeline* paraphrase while keeping the prompts verbatim.
2. **Paper footnote 1 links rot faster than the repos.** MedGENIE's published URL (`unibo-nlp/medgenie`) 404s; the live repo is `disi-unibo-nlp/medgenie`, recovered from the public record. Its evaluation inputs sit in a Google Drive zip (`data_ragas_eval.zip`) rather than in the repo or on Hugging Face, so the only in-repo-durable half of that claim is the generated contexts (mirrored on HF under MIT).
3. **"Raw CoT" is a new class of blocker.** W7134291344 is not blocked by private data, a dead snapshot or a human outcome — it is blocked because the measurement reads a channel the vendor does not expose. The authors state they obtained OpenAI raw CoTs through a collaboration with OpenAI, and their own released runner never requests reasoning for OpenAI models. Substituting API reasoning summaries would produce a number that is not the paper's metric. This is the first card in the study where an affiliation, not an artefact, is the missing dependency.
4. **The Tier B upgrade works.** W4406880877 already ran at prompt level (TPR 54.67 vs the paper's 54.0, original inside CI, USD 16.50 measured). The repo upgrade replaces a Table-20 transcription — which carried a PDF artefact, `</end of candidate text>` — with the authors' own prompt file, adds their `seed=42` and drops the Tier B `max_completion_tokens`, and extends coverage from the 150 AI-generated articles to all 300 so the published cell `54 (4)` can be reproduced as a TPR/FPR pair. Measured Tier B token counts make this the only card in the batch whose budget is grounded rather than estimated.
5. **Retiring snapshots keep showing up as instruments, not as objects of study.** Two of the four live arms are judges (MedGENIE's RAGAS evaluator, CoT-Control's GPT-5-mini grader) and a third (Qworld's nano backbone) is a generator inside an ablation. Only the detector card puts the dying model on the stand as the thing being measured — which also makes it the only card where "the model died" would directly retire a headline number.
