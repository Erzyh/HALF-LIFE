## 4. Recommendation

(b) with a clear label, (c) for the claim as published. The paper cannot be re-executed as published without institutional access: 61 of the 113 articles (54 %) are closed and they carry 90 of the 175 recoverable TEST pairs, and the OSF deposit holds neither the article sections nor the raw GPT-4 answers, so the PDFs/EPUBs would have to be re-obtained and the manual section extraction redone for every non-PMC paper. What is feasible now at zero access cost is an OA-subset run on the recoverable Q1-8 TEST pairs: n = 52 papers covering 85/175 pairs (48.6 %), with the 29 PMC papers (41 pairs) extractable by the authors' own scripted route and the other 23 requiring our own PDF/HTML extraction (18 of them from repository manuscripts rather than the version of record, which is a fidelity caveat because the Schulz labels were assigned on the published text). That subset can test the pooled claim (90 % accuracy, CI roughly +/-6-7 points) but not the per-item claims (7-16 pairs per question) and not item 9. If the user does not confirm institutional access, run the OA subset as a labelled "OA-subset, Q1-8 TEST" result, with the version-of-record subset (36 papers, 51 pairs) as the primary and all-OA (85 pairs) as a sensitivity analysis; if access is confirmed, the full 175-pair run needs 61 more full texts plus manual extraction for about 84 non-PMC papers.
