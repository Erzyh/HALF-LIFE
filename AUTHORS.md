## Contributions

H.G. conceived and designed the study, built the census, the re-execution harness and the deposit, ran the
re-executions, performed the statistical analyses and the visualizations, and wrote the first draft. S.A., J.H.,
Ju.K. and Jm.K. coded the census sample and judged the re-execution results, and reviewed and edited the manuscript.
S.K. co-conceived the study, supervised it, provided resources and funding, and critically revised the manuscript.
All authors approved the final version.
